The goal is to make priorities, owners, deadlines, dependencies, and risks easy to scan.

## Status Definitions

**Complete**  
The item is finalized and no additional action is currently required.

**In Progress**  
Work is underway and the item is being actively managed.

**Not Started**  
The item has not yet begun or is waiting on a dependency.

**Blocked**  
The item cannot move forward until another decision or action is completed.

## What I Watch Closely

The highest-risk planning items are usually the ones that affect multiple other tasks.

Examples include:

- Location approval
- Budget approval
- Final attendee count
- Travel information
- Agenda completion
- Contract deadlines
- Cancellation windows
- Catering guarantees

I track these closely because delays in one area can create problems across several others.

## Executive Escalation

I would only bring an item to leadership when executive judgment or approval is actually required.

Examples:

- Budget changes
- Final location selection
- Contract terms outside approved limits
- Major agenda decisions
- Significant schedule changes
- Vendor issues with financial or reputational impact

Routine follow-up, coordination, scheduling, and confirmation would stay with me.
